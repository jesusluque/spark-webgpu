←Relightable pawn

A chess pawn baked to 1.4M Gaussian splats, each carrying how it receives light. Pick an HDRI and the splats are relit on the GPU: dome, sun and bounce light, marble body and glass head. On a desktop, Maya's camera: Alt + left tumble, middle track, right dolly, F to frame; elsewhere drag to look.
