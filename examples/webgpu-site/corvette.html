←Relightable Corvette

A Corvette baked to Gaussian splats, each carrying how it receives light, thinned to 1.1M splats for the web. Pick an HDRI and the car is relit on the GPU: the dome with its shadows, an optional sun, the glass and the car’s shadow on the ground. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
